# Softphone

The browser-based softphone is available on every page in the operator panel. It uses WebRTC (SIP.js) to connect to Asterisk through the platform's WSS proxy.

## Overview

The softphone appears in the bottom area of the operator panel and persists across page navigation (SPA mode). Active calls are not interrupted when you switch between Workspace, Email Inbox, or other pages.
