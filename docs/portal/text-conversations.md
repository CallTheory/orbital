# Text Conversations

Text Conversations shows the client every SMS and MMS exchange handled on
their behalf — what their customers asked and what was said in reply.

Like the rest of the portal it is read-only.

## Reading a conversation

A conversation is one continuous thread between one of the client's numbers
and one customer. Opening it shows the whole exchange in order, with who
sent what:

- **The customer's messages**, as they sent them
- **Replies**, marked as sent by an operator or by an AI agent

A client can see when a person answered and when an agent did. That is
deliberate — it is their customer being spoken to, and they are entitled to
know who did the speaking.

## Attachments

Photographs and other media the customer sent appear inline. Orbital keeps
its own copy rather than linking to the carrier, so an attachment is still
there months later when the original link would long since have expired.

## Why a client cannot reply here

The portal shows the conversation; it is not a second place to join it.

If clients could reply directly, a customer would receive answers from two
different voices with neither aware of the other. Replies come from
operators so the thread stays coherent — and so that consent, opt-out, and
carrier compliance are enforced in one place rather than two.

A client who wants something said can ask the service to say it.

## Opted-out customers

A conversation may show that the customer has opted out of texts. That means
they replied STOP, and nobody — operator or agent — can text them again
until they restart the conversation themselves.

This is a legal requirement rather than a setting, which is why there is no
way to override it from either the portal or the operator side.
