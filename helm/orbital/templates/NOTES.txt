Or via port-forward (no DNS / TLS required):
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "orbital.componentName" (list . "laravel") }} 8000:{{ .Values.laravel.service.port }}

Image pull check:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} get events --field-selector reason=Failed
  (look for ImagePullBackOff — typically a missing or wrong-named
   `orbital-registry-creds` secret; see helm/README.md in this repo)
